Model-independent test of GR

With Arman Shafieloo and Hyungjin Kim (University of Waterloo), we combined model-independent reconstructions of the expansion history from supernovae and BAO with growth measurements from redshift-space distortions, and put model-independent constraints on the matter density {{< tex math=`\Omega_\mathrm{m}` >}}, the rms fluctuation {{< tex math=`\sigma_8` >}}, and the growth index {{< tex math=`\gamma` >}}, defined by

{{< tex math=`f(z) \equiv \frac{\mathrm{d}\ln\delta}{\mathrm{d}\ln a} \simeq \Omega_\mathrm{m}(z)^{\gamma},` block="true" >}}

with {{< tex math=`\gamma \simeq 0.55` >}} in GR. Our results are consistent with {{< tex math=`\Lambda` >}}CDM + GR (L'Huillier, Shafieloo & Kim 2018, MNRAS 476, 3263).

With Arman Shafieloo and Alexei Starobinsky, we then combined the latest type Ia supernova data (Pantheon) with growth measurements (including eBOSS DR14Q) and obtained more stringent constraints, still consistent with {{< tex math=`\Lambda` >}}CDM + GR (Shafieloo, L'Huillier & Starobinsky 2018, PRD 98, 083526).
