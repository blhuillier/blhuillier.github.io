Many quantities in cosmology are only observed through an integral: lensing data, for example, measure the matter power spectrum convolved with a geometric kernel. Recovering the underlying function is an inverse problem, ill-posed and sensitive to noise.

With J.-Q. Jiang, A. Dawn, D. K. Hazra and A. Shafieloo, we use modified and regularised Richardson–Lucy deconvolution to reconstruct the matter power spectrum, without assuming its shape, from CMB lensing and from 3×2pt measurements (galaxy clustering, galaxy–galaxy lensing and cosmic shear).
