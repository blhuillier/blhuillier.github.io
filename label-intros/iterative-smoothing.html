Iterative smoothing reconstructs a function, such as the distance–redshift relation of supernovae, without assuming a parametric form. Starting from a guess, it repeatedly smooths the error-weighted residuals between the data and the current reconstruction with a kernel, until the reconstruction fits the data. Starting from many different guesses gives an ensemble of reconstructions consistent with the data.

Because it does not rely on a model, it can test one: if no member of a model family falls within the ensemble, the model is ruled out. I use it to test the FLRW metric and spatial flatness, to measure H0rd without assuming a cosmological model, and, with my students and collaborators, to build likelihoods for model selection.
