Type Ia supernovae are standardisable candles: their distances trace the expansion history of the Universe out to redshift about 2. Most of my model-independent tests start from supernova compilations (JLA, Pantheon, Pantheon+ and DES), reconstructed with iterative smoothing or Gaussian processes.

Combined with baryon acoustic oscillations, growth or cluster data, these reconstructions test the flatness of the Universe, the FLRW metric and General Relativity. They also help diagnose systematics, such as the light-curve hyper-parameters or the Pantheon+ covariance matrix, and forecast what Rubin/LSST and Roman will achieve.
