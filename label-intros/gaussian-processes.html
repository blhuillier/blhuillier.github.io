Gaussian process regression reconstructs a smooth function, such as a distance, the Hubble rate or the growth rate, directly from data, with its uncertainties and without choosing a parametric form. I use it to reconstruct the expansion and growth histories and to derive dark energy and modified-gravity quantities from them.

Its results depend on the mean function and on the kernel hyperparameters. With my students, we showed how to choose them and marginalise over them, so that the reconstruction does not quietly assume the model it is meant to test.
